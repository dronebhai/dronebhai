## **FAQs** 

### **What are customized drones?** 

Customized drones are UAV systems engineered or modified to meet specific payload, endurance, sensor, communication or mission requirements. 

### **Can a drone be customized for a specific payload?** 

Yes. Payload mounting, power requirements, vibration isolation and flight configuration can be designed around the intended payload. 

### **Can you integrate specialized sensors with a custom drone?** 

Yes. Custom UAV platforms can be configured for specialized sensors and mission equipment depending on the application. 

### **Which flight controllers can be used for custom drones?** 

Custom platforms can be developed around supported flight stacks such as ArduPilot or PX4, depending on project requirements. 

### **Can customized drones be designed for continuous operation?** 

Yes. Tethered configurations can be designed for extended or continuous-hover applications where persistent airborne operation is required.
